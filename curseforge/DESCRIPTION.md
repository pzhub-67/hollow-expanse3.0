# The Hollow Expanse

Step through a portal of reinforced obsidian into a **void dimension of floating islands** lit by soft, living light.

## Features
- **A new dimension**: drifting glow-lit islands in tiers above an endless abyss. No sun, a faint starfield, and light motes in the air.
- **Tether & Anchor**: the signature mechanic. Grapple between islands with the **Tether Hook**, bind yourself to **Anchor Stones** (respawn + recall point), and use light as your lifeline: lingering in the dark inflicts **Fading** and shrinks your max health.
- **Falling is not dying**: drop off the bottom and you land in the **Underlayer**, a darker five-tier world with tougher mobs and richer ore. Climb the tiers with your tether to escape.
- **Voidsteel**: a glowing ore that grows from the *undersides* of islands. Tools faster than diamond, armor with **Weightless Step** (slow fall, no fall damage), and an upgraded Voidsteel Tether Hook. Fuse with netherite and a Warden's Core for **Hollow-forged** gear.
- **The Hollow Warden** (boss): three phases. Shard barrages, gravity wells that drag you in, then the arena shatters into small platforms while the Warden vanishes and strikes from nowhere.
- **Three enchantments**: *Gravity Anchor* (resist knockback and gravity pulls, doubled while sneaking), *Void Siphon* (kills restore hunger and health, stronger in the Hollow), *Echo Strike* (treasure; hits can repeat as a ghost attack).
- **Mobs**: gentle glowing **Drifters**, ambushing **Husk Stalkers**.
- Smooth, glowing textures throughout.

## Getting there
Craft Reinforced Obsidian and a Void Ignition Core, build a frame, light it, and step in. See the in-game recipe book or the README.

## Configuration
`config/hollow_expanse-common.toml`: fall to Underlayer, Fading on/off and timing, Warden health, arena crumbling, End-only portal.

## Requirements
Minecraft 1.21.1, Forge 52.x, Java 21.
